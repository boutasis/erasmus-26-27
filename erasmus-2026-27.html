Digital Citizenship & Online Responsibility

Be a Mindful Digital Citizen:

  • • Practice digital empathy—treat people online with the same respect, kindness, and decency you would face-to-face.
  • • Stand up against cyberbullying, hate speech, and online harassment instead of being a silent bystander.

Fight Misinformation & Fake News:

  • • Pause before sharing: verify facts and check reliable sources before hitting "share" or "retweet."
  • • Understand how algorithms work to avoid getting trapped in online echo chambers that feed you biased information.

Protect Privacy & Data Rights:

  • • Respect other people’s privacy—never share photos, personal details, or private messages without explicit consent.
  • • Take control of your own digital footprint by keeping security settings updated and protecting your online identity.

Promote Constructive Online Dialogue:

  • • Use social media as a tool to build community, raise awareness about global issues, and foster positive change rather than division.
  • • Listen to differing opinions in comment sections with an open mind and debate ideas respectfully.
